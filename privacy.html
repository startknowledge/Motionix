Motionix (operated by StartKnowledge) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and any other details you include in messages sent to us via contact forms or email.

2. Automatically Collected Data

Like many websites, we automatically collect certain information when you visit, including:

We use Cookies Policy and similar tracking technologies. For more information, please see our Cookies Policy.

We use the information we collect to:
