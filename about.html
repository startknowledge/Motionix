About Motionix

We create premium, ready-to-use GSAP animation templates for developers and designers worldwide.

Home  ›  About

Our Mission

Motionix was founded with a simple goal: to make sophisticated web animations accessible to everyone. We believe that animation is a powerful storytelling tool, and with the right resources, any developer or designer can create stunning, high-performance animations that captivate users.

Our templates are built on GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform), the industry-standard library for high-performance JavaScript animations. Each template is meticulously crafted to be clean, well-documented, and easy to integrate into any project.

We are passionate about quality and performance. Every template is tested across devices and browsers to ensure a smooth experience.

What We Offer

  • Ready-to-use components: Button hover effects, scroll-triggered animations, loader animations, and more.
  • GSAP-powered: All templates leverage the power of GSAP for smooth, hardware-accelerated animations.
  • Developer-friendly: Clean, commented code with easy customization options.
  • Responsive: Animations work flawlessly on all screen sizes.
  • Regular updates: We continuously improve our library with new templates and features.

Our Team

Motionix is a project by StartKnowledge, a collective of developers, designers, and creatives dedicated to building innovative digital products. Our team has years of experience in web development, animation, and user experience design.

We are a remote team spread across the globe, united by a shared love for clean code and beautiful animations. We believe in open communication, continuous learning, and delivering value to our users.

Why Choose Motionix?

  • Save Time: Skip the trial-and-error and start with proven, production-ready animations.
  • Learn from the Best: Our templates are built by GSAP experts, so you can learn best practices by studying the code.
  • Commercial License: Use our templates in personal and commercial projects without any additional fees.
  • Community Support: Join our growing community of developers and get help when you need it.

Our Commitment to Quality

We stand behind the quality of our products. Each template undergoes a rigorous review process to ensure it meets our high standards. We also welcome feedback from our users to help us continuously improve.

If you ever encounter an issue, our support team is here to help. We pride ourselves on fast, friendly, and effective customer service.
