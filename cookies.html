What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to help websites function properly, remember preferences, and provide analytics. They are widely used to improve user experience.

How We Use Cookies

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the basic functioning of the website (e.g., security, session management).
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings (e.g., language, theme).
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies: Used to deliver relevant ads (if applicable).

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services like Google Analytics and AdSense, which set their own cookies. You can review their privacy policies for more details.
